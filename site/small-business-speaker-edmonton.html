Small business speaker in Edmonton.

Short answer

George Ogunbande speaks to Edmonton business owners and founders on building a brand on a small budget and learning from expensive mistakes. He is the Founder and CEO of FlatPurse and the author of Small Money, Big Brand and Tales Told by an Idiot.

By George Ogunbande, author and founder of FlatPurse · Edmonton, Alberta · Updated October 2026

What George speaks about

  • Small Money, Big Brand: Think. Solve. Act. The practical method behind his book on building a brand on a tight budget. See the book.
  • Learning from expensive mistakes. Honest stories of business screwups and the rules they left behind, drawn from Tales Told by an Idiot.
  • Building a company. What it takes to start with a real problem and build something useful, from his work at FlatPurse.
