FlatPurse.

I started FlatPurse to build practical technology for the everyday work of running a business. The rule is simple: start with a real problem, then build something useful.

Our first product, Flow, comes from a problem every salon and barbershop owner knows: empty chairs. No-shows, last-minute gaps and clients who quietly stop coming back cost a small shop more than any software subscription. Flow is built to win that time back.

Speaker bio

George Ogunbande is an entrepreneur, human resources professional and author of Small Money, Big Brand, Tales Told by an Idiot and the children’s book Maya the Mini Mogul. He is the Founder and CEO of FlatPurse, which builds booking and retention software for service businesses, and brings experience in people management, business development and board governance. George holds a degree in Labour Studies and Human Resources Management from the University of Windsor and a People Analytics certificate from Wharton. His work explores leadership, entrepreneurship and the practical steps people can take to turn ideas into meaningful action.
