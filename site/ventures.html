FlatPurse.

I started FlatPurse to build practical technology for the everyday work of running a business. The rule is simple: start with a real problem, then build something useful.

Our first product, Flow, comes from a problem every salon and barbershop owner knows: empty chairs. No-shows, last-minute gaps and clients who quietly stop coming back cost a small shop more than any software subscription. Flow is built to win that time back.
